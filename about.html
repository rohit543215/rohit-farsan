Our Story

Rohit Farsan House was founded with a simple vision: to bring the authentic taste of traditional Gujarati farsan and snacks to every household. What started as a small family business has grown into a beloved destination for those seeking fresh, high-quality farsan made with time-honored recipes passed down through generations.

Every product that leaves our kitchen is made with love, care, and the finest ingredients. We believe in maintaining the authenticity of flavors while ensuring the highest standards of hygiene and quality. Our customers' trust and satisfaction are the cornerstones of our success.

Our Mission

To preserve and promote the rich culinary heritage of Gujarat by offering authentic, fresh, and delicious farsan and snacks that bring joy to every celebration and everyday moment. We are committed to quality, tradition, and customer satisfaction in everything we do.
